Cyber Security

I help businesses build strong security postures through risk assessment, policy development, and security awareness training. My goal is to create resilient organizations that can prevent, detect, and respond to cyber incidents effectively.

  • Risk Assessment
    Identifying weaknesses in your systems, networks, and processes before attackers do.
  • Policy Development
    Building clear security policies, procedures, and incident response plans.
  • Security Awareness
    Training your staff to recognize phishing, social engineering, and common threats.
  • Monitoring & Response
    Helping you detect, contain, and recover from cyber incidents quickly.
  • Compliance
    Aligning your security practices with standards like ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, and GDPR.
