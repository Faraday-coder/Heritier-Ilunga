Penetration Testing

Conduct authorized security assessments of web apps, networks, and systems. Perform reconnaissance, vulnerability scanning, and controlled exploitation using tools like Burp Suite, Nmap, and Metasploit. Document findings, rate risks, write technical and executive reports, and recommend remediation. Collaborate with teams and retest fixes to strengthen security posture.

  • Reconnaissance
    Passive and active information gathering — OSINT, DNS enumeration, subdomain discovery.
  • Vulnerability Assessment
    Manual and automated testing for OWASP Top 10 and beyond.
  • Exploitation
    Controlled exploitation to validate findings and measure real-world impact.
  • Reporting
    Clear technical and executive reports with CVSS risk ratings and PoC evidence.
  • Retesting
    Verifying that fixes work and no new vulnerabilities were introduced.
  • Tools I Use
    Burp Suite, Metasploit, Nmap, sqlmap, Wireshark, Nessus, BloodHound.
